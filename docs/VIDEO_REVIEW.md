# SportsRivalry video production preferences

User instruction (October 10, 2026): perform two grading passes for every video before showing it.

1. Review the initial cut for hook strength, narrative clarity, pacing, evidence, payoff and audience engagement. Record scores out of 10 and concrete observations in the project’s grading-report.md. Fix weaknesses before the next pass.
2. Independently review the revised finished video for engagement plus visual framing, caption timing/readability, source accuracy, narration, music balance and ending. Check actual rendered frames and audio; record second-pass scores, fixes and any remaining limits.

Do not claim scores predict views or that an automated check substitutes for editorial review. Show the video only after both passes are complete. Keep the reports with the editable project.

SportsRivalry covers all sports. Reuse the approved charcoal/red design, word-highlight captions and local British female voice bf_emma when the user asks for the same style. Keep opening music audible, narration clear, and omit the bottom footage/edit credit line.
